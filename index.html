Position Sizing Cheat Sheet

Recommended risk per trade by expiry.

ExpiryRisk %$ amountNotes
0–2 days (pure lotto)0.5–1%High theta, fast death — max 1%
7 days (medium speed)1–2%Theta hurts, but room for 200–500%
30–60 days (main lotto)5–10%Time + gamma = big wins. TP half at 100%

Always take profit on half at +100% — then it's a free ride. No more than 1% on anything under 7 days.
