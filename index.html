Plan B · Liquidity Sweep

Pre-commit before the open
  • Trigger: Plan A’s breakout fails; price sweeps a prior low and reclaims it on a 5-minute close. Enter on the reclaim, not during the sweep.
  • Entry: pick the strike whose premium is in the $0.25–$0.40 mid-range at the time of the trigger — the strike number is not fixed (a morning sweep and an afternoon sweep may need different strikes). Direction follows the day’s map (call on a call day).
  • Stop: tight, just below the sweep low.
  • Targets: +100% take half, move stop to breakeven on the rest, runner to +300%.
  • Rules: Plan B at most once per day. If Plan A loses, Plan B is the last plan for the day (the 2-trades-a-day max still applies). Define Plan B before the open alongside Plan A — improvising it mid-session after Plan A is bleeding counts as chasing, not executing.

🔺 Pyramid · Free-money chain

Added Nov 12, 2026
  • Size: 4–6 contracts per trade until the next subway station.
  • Start: a pyramid can only start after one of the day's 2 trades hits +100% and 50% is taken off. Fund it only with that realized profit — no new risk.
  • Chain: each pyramid is a new trade (call or put, 0DTE or 1DTE), with no time or price rule and not tied to older trades. Every pyramid that hits +100% (50% off) can fund the next one with its own profit. No limit on the chain, and pyramids don't count toward the 2-trade max.
  • End: the chain stops the moment one pyramid loses. Anti-FOMO: no new pyramid after a losing pyramid.
  • Directional, not vega: every pyramid has a defined stop and target. Example: pick a premium, set a stop around −40%, and a target around +100%.
  • Call + put together: the put is the main directional position, the call is insurance. Never stop out both at once; if the call stops, keep the put. Set the call's stop higher so it works as insurance.
  • Kites: after +100% on the put, the leftover put is a kite, closed before 3:30 PM ET, never overnight. For a call kite, raise the stop after the partial to lock gains but leave room to run.
  • Order discipline: lock the stop and target orders in place; don't move them by hand.

⏱️ Late Scalp Setup

Afternoon setup, separate from the morning playbook. Log saved only in this browser.

📊 Easier: import your Wealthsimple CSV in the Trade Journal → Setup Compare — it splits Late Scalp vs Morning automatically. This manual log is optional.
  • Entry: 3:00–3:30 PM ET (scrub / Late Scalp window). Entering is optional — no trade is fine if nothing sets up.
  • Out by: 3:30 PM ET — flat before the last half hour.
  • After 3:30: no new positions (exit-only until the close).
  • Strike: within 1 point of the current price.
  • Premium: about $0.20–$0.35.
  • Size: 2–4 contracts.
  • Direction: follow the day's map (puts when the map favors puts).
  • Pin-risk: after 3:00 PM ET, an open 0DTE kite within 1 pt of strike may get pinned into the close — see the Trade Journal pin-risk banner (unlock required).

🪁 Kite Management

Protective floor on remaining runner contracts after partial profits. Trail log saved only in this browser. Same rules apply on the Trade Journal.

  • Initial floor: after taking partial profits, place a manual protective limit sell on the remaining kite at ~50% of the current kite mark.
  • Trail up: every time the kite doubles, cancel the old limit and place a new one at ~50% of the new mark.
  • Runner size: keep the runner to 1 contract per position.
  • Hard stop: all kites closed by 3:30 PM ET (same hard stop as Late Scalp / Playbook).
  • Log every trail: record mark, new floor, time (ET), and action in the journal below.
  • Pin-risk: after 3:00 PM ET, an open 0DTE kite within 1 pt of strike may get pinned — see the Trade Journal pin-risk banner (unlock required).
